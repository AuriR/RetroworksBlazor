> Keyboard symbol **⌘** below represents the Apple/Open-Apple key shown in the original.

## The Clipboards

When you move or copy information to the Clipboard, the information is placed on the Clipboard associated with that module. When you print to the Clipboard (or have the category rules or spelling summary placed on the Clipboard), the information is placed on the Word Processor Clipboard.

When you move or copy from the Clipboard, the information comes from the last Clipboard information was placed on. Hold down **⌘** while selecting **From Clipboard** to move or copy information from a different Clipboard.
